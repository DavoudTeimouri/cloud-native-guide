    10 · Upgrade and Lifecycle Management¶

    Purpose and scope¶

    Document controlled upgrade procedures for every infrastructure component: OS, firmware, hypervisors, Kubernetes, Kubespray, Rancher, CNI, CSI, add-ons, Argo CD, Ceph, Rook-Ceph, storage appliances, load balancers, GitLab, Nexus, Harbor, monitoring. Includes version compatibility, upgrade order, prerequisites, backup, pre-upgrade validation, execution, post-upgrade validation, rollback strategy, downtime considerations, air-gapped upgrade procedures.

    Subsections¶

    Subsection Covers
    OS, Firmware, Hypervisor OS patches, firmware, hypervisor upgrades; compatibility, backup, rollback
    Kubernetes & Kubespray K8s and Kubespray version upgrades; upgrade order, etcd backup, one control-plane node at a time
    CNI, CSI, Add-ons, Argo CD CNI/CSI/add-on/Argo CD upgrades; compatibility, rollback
    Ceph, Rook-Ceph, Storage Appliances Ceph/Rook/storage appliance upgrades; expansion, upgrade, troubleshooting
    Load Balancer, GitLab, Nexus, Harbor LB, GitLab, Nexus, Harbor upgrades; TLS, backup/restore
    Monitoring Components Prometheus, Grafana, Alertmanager, Loki upgrades
    Compatibility, Order, Backup, Rollback Version compatibility, upgrade order, backup, pre/post validation, rollback strategy, downtime
    Air-Gapped Upgrade Upgrade procedures for fully disconnected environments

    • Rollback/restore procedures in 08 · Troubleshooting and 11 · Maintenance
    • Air-gapped artifact handling in 03 · Repository & Registry Management
