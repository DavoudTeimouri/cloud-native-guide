# GitLab Images

## Purpose and scope

GitLab application container images. Lists public upstream address and internal mirrored Harbor address.

## Image addresses

| Component | Image prefix | Public upstream address | Internal mirrored address | Version source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GitLab | `registry.gitlab.com/gitlab/gitlab-ee` | registry.gitlab.com | harbor.internal:5000 | version-matrix |
| GitLab Runner | `registry.gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab-runner` | registry.gitlab.com | harbor.internal:5000 | version-matrix |

## Validation

- GitLab container starts from harbor.internal:5000
- GitLab Runner registers against internal GitLab
- Container registry integrated with internal Harbor
