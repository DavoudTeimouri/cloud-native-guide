# Infrastructure Sizing and Resource Calculation

## Purpose and scope
Practical sizing methodology for physical servers, VMs, CPU, memory, storage capacity and performance, network bandwidth, control-plane nodes, workers, Ceph roles, load balancers, repositories, GitLab, monitoring/logging, and operational servers. Minimum, recommended, and production configurations.

## Sizing guidance
| Component | Minimum | Recommended | Production |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control-plane node | 2 vCPU, 8 GB RAM | 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM | 8 vCPU, 32 GB RAM |
| Worker node | 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM | 8 vCPU, 32 GB RAM | 16 vCPU, 64 GB RAM |
| Ceph OSD node | 4 vCPU, 32 GB | 8 vCPU, 64 GB | 16 vCPU, 128 GB |
| Load balancer | 2 vCPU, 4 GB | 4 vCPU, 8 GB | 8 vCPU, 16 GB |
| Repository/registry | 4 vCPU, 16 GB | 8 vCPU, 32 GB | 16 vCPU, 64 GB |
| GitLab | 4 vCPU, 8 GB | 8 vCPU, 16 GB | 16 vCPU, 32 GB |
| Monitoring server | 4 vCPU, 16 GB | 8 vCPU, 32 GB | 16 vCPU, 64 GB |
| Automation server | 2 vCPU, 8 GB | 4 vCPU, 16 GB | 8 vCPU, 32 GB |

## etcd storage
etcd latency is disk latency. Use dedicated local NVMe, never a shared filesystem. Monitor `etcd_disk_backend_commit_duration_seconds`; p99 above 25 ms means disk is the bottleneck.

## Capacity planning
- Resource reservations for kubelet and system components.
- Growth estimates, failure tolerance, overcommit considerations.
- Ceph 3x replication consumes roughly 2x client write bandwidth on replication path.
- Measure fabric before sizing storage; iperf3 between OSD hosts establishes real ceiling.
